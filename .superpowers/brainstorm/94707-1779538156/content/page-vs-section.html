Where should the Produce section live?

This affects navigation, discoverability, and how much space the catalogue + form gets

A

Dedicated page — /produce

A full standalone page added to the nav. Plenty of room for the full produce catalogue, category filters, and the bulk request form. Visitors can bookmark or share it directly.

Pros

  • All the space you need
  • Linkable & shareable
  • Nav item signals it's a real offering

Cons

  • One more page to maintain
B

Section on the homepage

Added below the current homepage sections. Visitors see it while scrolling. No new nav entry needed.

Pros

  • Zero nav change needed
  • Casual visitors stumble on it

Cons

  • Homepage already has 4 sections
  • Form + catalogue = lot of content crammed in
  • Can't link directly to it cleanly
C

Both — teaser on homepage + full page

A short "From our farm" teaser block on the homepage with a "See all produce" link to a full /produce page that has the complete catalogue and form.

Pros

  • Best of both worlds
  • Homepage stays lean

Cons

  • Most build work
